Driving licence in Barcelona

Four years of residence is more than the six months required: you can start now.

The plan

  1. Medical report at a centro de reconocimiento de conductores (valid 90 days).
  2. Sign up with a driving school. It is required for the driving lessons.
  3. Pay the DGT fee: 94.05 €, covers two exam attempts.
  4. Theory exam, in Spanish: 30 questions, 3 mistakes allowed.
  5. Driving lessons, then the practical exam.

Cost

≈ 616 € + medical report

Cheapest of the offers checked: Alba Autoescola (Eixample), Pack A at 522 €: enrolment, theory, 5 lessons, first practical exam. Plus the DGT fee. Extra lessons or retakes cost more.

Free, worth doing

Register as a jobseeker with the SOC, by phone:

930 886 200

Mon–Fri, 8:30–14:00. Under 30, this also enrols you in Garantía Juvenil (youth job programmes). No aid paying for the licence was found, but ask.
